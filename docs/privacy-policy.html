Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker (“the App”) is an offline personal finance tool. Effective date: 6 August 2026.

Data we process

Transaction details you import (amounts, merchants, categories, optional SMS text you paste or share, notes, and backup files you create) are stored only on your device in a local database.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, or advertising ID
  • No internet permission and no analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal data
  • No cloud sync unless you export a backup file and upload it yourself (e.g. Google Drive via the system file picker)

SMS

Store / Play build (com.expensetracker.offline): the App does not request READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. You may paste message text or share text into the App.

SMS / Galaxy Store build (com.expensetracker.offline.sms, listed as Offline Expense Tracker): the App may request READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED solely to import banking and UPI payment alerts into a local ledger. SMS content is parsed and stored only on your device. The App has no internet permission, so message text cannot be uploaded by the App.

Permissions are optional to grant; without them you can still add expenses manually or paste message text.

Backups

Backups are JSON files you choose to create or restore. Treat them as sensitive (they may include original SMS text). Device auto-backup of the App database is disabled.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository hosting this policy, or contact the developer who published the store listing.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in this document and the App version notes.
